The paper — The Signal, Not the Scheduler

English · 한국어 · 中文. Four figures. Every number in it is bound to a committed artifact by a test, so the prose cannot drift away from the run that produced it.

In one sentence. On a dependency graph with a fixed review budget, the part a policy controls — which claim to open next — is nearly exhausted; the part it cannot control — which claim looks suspicious — is where the remaining error lives.

Why that is a result and not a shrug. Both halves are measured against the same frozen splits. The structure half fails the same way in every cell we ran; the signal half fails in a way a reviewer can feel: the flag points at consequences, and the reviewer's own inputs are the causes.

What the real data can say

13,990 daily sleep records from 237 participants, with 75 human-confirmed corrections — 0.54 % of records. The defects cluster by type: 57 of the 75 (76 %) are times written in the wrong order. A defect does not stay put; it becomes a wrong latency, a wrong efficiency, a wrong conclusion.

The row-level review queue holds 68 human-confirmed positives at a 6.5 % base rate. The same confidence flag scores 0.966 on the four validity sinks, 0.733 on the six derived nodes, and 0.569 on the four raw timestamps a reviewer would actually open.

The queue is bounded, not badly scheduled. 90.3 % of the reachable errors are in it at all, but only 83.8 % can be reached by k = 140, because 16.2 % sit in 885 rows at a 1.2 % base rate. That is a detector problem; no ordering fixes it.

What is not claimed

No budget headline. "RL reduces human review by X %" appears nowhere: that phrasing came from legacy seeds 1–20 and reproduced nowhere. RETIRED_NUMBERS.md lists it with every other retired value, and a test fails the build if one returns.

No deployed saving. The sleep corrections are replayed, not rerun with a policy in the loop.

No per-node probabilities. Naming the cause of a row needs audited-correct rows, which the lab has not supplied; node-level calibration is out of scope until they arrive.

The mechanism is a hypothesis. That multi-hop propagation has to be re-derived from terminal reward explains the structural failure, but this data does not separate it from alternatives.

Where the numbers come from

Three checks run on a clean clone: test_published_numbers.py (19) binds each published number to an artifact, test_figures.py (6) re-derives every plotted value, and test_retired_numbers.py (3) keeps superseded values out of every tracked markdown file. The three drafts carry the same 630 numeric tokens.

Raw run outputs are not in the repository: *.rds and *.csv stay out of git, and only aggregates and calibration.json are committed.
