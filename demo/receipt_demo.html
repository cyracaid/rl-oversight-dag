What a delivery can say about its own coverage

Same review log, same policy, same episodes. Three ways of describing the result. One of them makes a question answerable.

silent

Reports the review log and stops. No field says what was missed.
no claim

overclaim

Every reviewed sink is reported clean, so nothing is reported missed.
wrong

honest receipt

Says inspected, partial or untouched per sink, and counts the errors outside full coverage.
accurate scope
