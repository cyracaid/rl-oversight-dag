Ablation: GNN+REINFORCE under three confidence conditions (3 seeds x 12k ep)
  normal = learned conf signal (as in main experiments)
  noise  = conf replaced by uniform noise (signal destroyed)
  oracle = conf reveals the true error (upper bound of the signal)

  seed 1  normal  1.30
  seed 1  noise   3.30
  seed 1  oracle  0.80
  seed 2  normal  1.95
  seed 2  noise   5.20
  seed 2  oracle  0.70
  seed 3  normal  0.90
  seed 3  noise   2.85
  seed 3  oracle  0.65
